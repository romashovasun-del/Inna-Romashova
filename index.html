Landing Page Design

One page with one clear goal — from the first wireframe to a live site.

Includes: structure and copy outline, wireframe, final design in Figma, mobile version. Optional: turnkey build in Webflow or vibe-coded.

Website Build

From design to launch — or straight to the build if you already have a layout.

How I work

  1. 1

    Brief

    We define the goal, audience and scope. You get a clear plan and timeline before any design starts.

  2. 2

    Concept

    I explore directions and present one or two that fit the brief, with reasoning — not a pile of options.

  3. 3

    Design

    We refine the chosen direction together, usually over two rounds of feedback.

  4. 4

    Build & Launch

    I build the site (or prepare print files), test it and hand over everything you need.

About

I’m Inna, a brand and web designer with 7+ years of experience. I started in graphic design — logos, packaging, print — and moved into web because clients kept asking for both. Now most of my projects run from identity to a live site.

I’ve designed visual identities, marketing materials and digital products for healthcare, MedTech, e-learning, and natural beauty and home care brands — including Helsi, Choice, Lexus and Ukraine’s 30th Independence Anniversary — and delivered work for clients from the UK and Switzerland.

I work in English, Ukrainian and Russian with clients in Europe and beyond.
